# Infinitility: your digital partner, without limits

**Most company websites are built once and then left alone. Infinitility is the opposite: a living, bilingual site that draws itself in front of the visitor and then keeps moving, like the business it represents.**

Infinitility Tech Ltd designs, builds and runs the digital side of a business: hosting and domains, web and app development, design, marketing, AI, social media, APIs and cloud. This site is where that story begins.

🔗 **Repository:** https://github.com/fabughali/infinitility

## ✨ What makes it different

- **The "Phoenix ∞" motion system.** The logo is a phoenix whose body is an infinity loop, and the whole site moves with it. The hero draws a glowing ∞ stroke by stroke, with sparks flowing along the curve, a drifting star field, and depth that follows the mouse.
- **Truly bilingual.** Arabic is the default and fully right-to-left, and English is one tap away. Every layout, arrow and animation mirrors correctly, set in the Readex Pro typeface designed for both scripts.
- **Animation with manners.** Sections rise into view, numbers count up, service cards trace their borders on hover, and headlines rotate through words with a soft blur. Every effect respects the visitor's "reduce motion" setting, and the endless loops pause when they're off screen or the tab is hidden, to save battery.
- **Built for every screen.** The layout adapts from a wide desktop down to a phone, with quick WhatsApp and call buttons always within reach.
- **A control room behind the curtain.** A secure, bilingual admin panel organised by service lets the team change every text, image and contact detail, preview the result on the live site, publish, and roll back any earlier version. Enquiries land in a built-in inbox.
- **Security first.** Staff sign in with 2-step verification. Each team member only sees the sections they are responsible for, and service keys are stored encrypted, never in the code.

## 🛠️ Under the hood

- **Flutter web** (Dart 3) for pixel-perfect rendering and smooth 60 fps motion
- **Custom painters** for the ∞ hero, header wave and dividers, with no video and no heavy animation libraries
- **go_router** for clean, shareable URLs on every page
- **Flutter localization** (ARB) for complete Arabic and English text
- A custom-built **∞ loading screen** that shows instantly while the app starts
- **Supabase** for sign-in with 2-step verification, the database with row-level security, encrypted key storage and image hosting
- **Always on**: if the backend is ever slow or unreachable, visitors still get the full site instantly
- **Tested**: over a hundred automated tests cover the infinity geometry, both languages, the contact form, content editing, permissions and the admin panel
